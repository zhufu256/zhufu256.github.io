Blessing Atusasibwe

Actor · Martial Artist · Voice Performer — Kyenjojo, Uganda

Bio

Blessing Atusasibwe (stage name Long-Brus Lee, alias Zhufu) is a Ugandan actor and martial artist based in Kyenjojo District. Drawing on Bafumbira heritage and Jeet Kune Do training, he brings physical discipline and quiet intensity to screen and stage work. He is also a self-taught web developer and the builder of the Ngezi K.D.H.S. Study Portal.

Seeking: film, television, commercial, and voice work. Languages: English, Runyankole.
